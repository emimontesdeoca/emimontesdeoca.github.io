## About me

I'm **Emiliano Montesdeoca**, a Uruguayan-Spanish software developer, **Microsoft MVP in Developer Technologies**, and proud dad based in **Tenerife, Canary Islands**.

I love tackling complex technical challenges and building scalable cloud solutions with Microsoft technologies. My daily toolkit revolves around **.NET**, **Azure**, **AI with Semantic Kernel**, and modern architectures like **.NET Aspire**.

## What I do

As **Cloud Solutions Team Lead** at [Intelequia Technologies](https://intelequia.com), I lead the design and delivery of cloud-native applications — blending strategic vision with hands-on execution. I thrive at the intersection of architecture and code, ensuring our team delivers solutions that are both elegant and production-ready.

## Community

I'm a frequent international speaker, having been recognized as **Sessionize Most Active Speaker** in [2023](https://sessionize.com/emimontesdeoca/), [2024](https://sessionize.com/emimontesdeoca/), and [2025](https://sessionize.com/emimontesdeoca/). I share practical, real-world insights through talks at conferences worldwide and through my blogs.

I'm also the creator of [**The .NET Blog**](https://thedotnetblog.com) — a resource for the .NET community — and I write regularly on this blog about the things I learn and build.

Mentoring and community building are a core part of who I am. Giving back to the ecosystem that shaped my career is something I take seriously.
